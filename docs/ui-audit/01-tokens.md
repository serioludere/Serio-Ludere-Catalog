## Dimension A — Visual Primitives

Swept all 10 sheets in `src/styles/` (5,562 lines) plus all 23 `.astro` files carrying a scoped `<style>` block. Every line number below was re-confirmed with `grep -n` / `sed -n 'Np'` immediately before writing.

### The headline

**Colour is solved. Motion, tracking, stacking and admin spacing are not.**

The codebase is far better tokenised than a raw grep suggests — but the discipline is _unevenly distributed by sheet, not by realm_:

| Sheet                                  | Lines | Literal spacing decls       | Literal colours | Verdict         |
| -------------------------------------- | ----- | --------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| `components.css`                       | 1,993 | **1** (`76px` iOS tab bar)  | 0               | exemplary       |
| `controls.css`                         | 326   | **1** (`-1px` sr-only clip) | 0               | exemplary       |
| `admin.css`                            | 768   | **26**                      | 0               | **the problem** |
| admin `.astro` scoped blocks (9 files) | —     | **18**                      | 1               | **the problem** |

`components.css` and `controls.css` prove the token system is sufficient to build against. `admin.css` — the one sheet in the one realm where work will actually land — ignores it. That is the single worst problem: **the 4pt scale exists, is complete, and admin does not use it.** 26 literal spacing declarations across 16 unique values, 10 of which (`1 2 5 6 7 10 14 18 26 28`) are off the 4pt grid entirely, sitting next to 12 correctly-tokenised `var(--rule-width)` uses in the same file.

### Verified clean (do not re-raise)

- **Zero primitive-token reads from any component.** A strict grep for every primitive name in `modes.css:21-186` (`--color-*`, `--size-body-*`, `--size-h*`, `--space-N`, `--space-deviation-*`, `--font-family-*`, `--font-weight-*`, `--radius-none/full`, `--border-hairline`, `--border-focus-width`, `--duration-*`, `--easing-*`, `--opacity-*`, `--ios-*`) outside `modes.css` returns **nothing**. The `modes.css:19` contract holds absolutely. (`--size-label` / `--size-mono` / `--size-icon` look primitive but are semantic, declared at `modes.css:265-267`.)
- **Colour:** only 15 unique literals exist outside the two token sheets, and 6 of those are inside comments. `border-radius` has 2 literals total. Both categories are effectively done.

### What is MISSING from the semantic tier

The 63 tokens pinned by `tests/unit/styles/token-cascade.test.ts` cover colour, type size, space, shape and two motion aliases. They cover **no** tracking, **no** stacking order, **no** elevation, **no** control height, and only two of nine line-heights in use. Those four gaps account for most of what follows.

### Counts

**23 findings** — 14 admin, 6 shared, 2 public, 1 customer. 2× P0, 12× P1, 9× P2.
